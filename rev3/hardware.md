For taildragger, drop the 4 M3×12, the 1 M3×20 and the 4 nuts of the trike nose gear. A 300-piece M3 assortment plus about 50 extra M3×10 covers it. Buy 10% spares.

Flight electronics are not included above:
- FC: 4 M3 nylon standoffs.
- Pi Zero 2: 4 M2.5.
- VTX: 4 M2 or M3.
- GPS and antenna: M3 or zip ties.

## Boom clamp access

The 2 boom clamp bolts sit inside the fuselage. Each side plate has a 7 mm access hole at those two points, so a 2.5 mm hex driver reaches the bolt head after the box is assembled. The nut is captive on the far side.
