# Rev 4 hardware list

All screws are M3 socket-head unless noted. Every screw into a printed part has a captive M3 hex nut in a hex pocket, so push the nut in before you fit the part. Lengths assume 1.0 mm sides, a 1.5 mm bottom, a 2 mm printed middle deck and a 1.0 mm upper deck.

For taildragger, drop 4 M3×12, 1 M3×20 and 4 hex nuts (the trike nose gear). Buy about 10% spares.

Flight electronics are not included above:
- FC: 4 M3, which self-tap into the printed standoffs.
- Pi Zero 2: 4 M2.5, which self-tap into its standoffs.
- VTX: 4 M2 or M3.
- GPS and antenna: M3 or zip ties.
